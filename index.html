Welcome

The ITAlian COnference on Survey Methodology (ITACOSM) is a biennial international conference organized by the Survey Sampling Group (S2G) of the Italian Statistical Society (SIS). As at the 2025 meeting in Bologna, S2G and the International Association of Survey Statisticians (IASS) join forces to organize ITACOSM-IASS 2027, which will be held at the Cremona meeting.

The main theme of this edition is Data Abundance, Valid Inference: The Role of Surveys. The conference welcomes contributions on survey sampling, estimation, data integration, small area estimation and statistical disclosure control, and on their applications in official statistics, economics, the social sciences, demography, the life and environmental sciences, and beyond.

More on S2G: s2gsis.github.io
More on IASS: isi-iass.org

Further details on the call for papers, the programme and registration will be published on this site in the coming months.
