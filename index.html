Welcome

The ITACOSM-IASS 2027 Conference will be held in Cremona, Italy, from 29 June to 2 July 2027, at the Cremona campus of the Università Cattolica del Sacro Cuore.

The main theme of this edition is Data Abundance, Valid Inference: The Role of Surveys. The conference welcomes contributions on survey sampling, estimation, data integration, small area estimation and statistical disclosure control, and on their applications in official statistics, economics, the social sciences and beyond.

Further details on the call for papers, the programme and registration will be published on this site in the coming months.
