The city

Cremona lies on the north bank of the river Po, in southern Lombardy. Its medieval centre is built around Piazza del Duomo, with the Romanesque Cathedral, the octagonal Baptistery, the Palazzo Comunale and the Torrazzo, the bell tower that dominates the city skyline and is among the tallest brick towers in Europe.

The city is world-famous for violin making. The tradition of the Amati, Guarneri and Stradivari workshops is still alive today in the many luthiers working in town, and the traditional violin craftsmanship of Cremona is inscribed on UNESCO's list of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity. Cremona is also known for its food: torrone, mostarda, cured meats and cheeses.

The campus

The Università Cattolica has been present in Cremona since 1984, with programmes in agricultural, food and environmental sciences and in economics. Since the 2020/21 academic year, teaching and research take place in the restored former convent of Santa Monica, a historic building in the city centre.

How to get there

By plane

The nearest international airports are:

  • Milan Linate (LIN) and Milan Malpensa (MXP), with connections to Milano Centrale railway station
  • Bergamo Orio al Serio (BGY)
  • Verona Villafranca (VRN)
  • Bologna Guglielmo Marconi (BLQ)
  • Parma Giuseppe Verdi (PMF)

From all of them, Cremona is best reached by train or by car.

By train

Cremona railway station has direct regional services from Milan and from Brescia, and connections to Mantua and Piacenza. Timetables and tickets: Trenord and Trenitalia.

The campus is about a 10-minute walk from the station: follow Via Dante to Piazza Risorgimento, then take Via Montello and Via Grado; after about 300 metres turn left into Via Bissolati. The university is on the right.

By bus

Urban bus line U connects the railway station and the Piazzale Atleti Azzurri d'Italia car park with the Santa Monica Campus.

By car

From the A21 motorway (Turin – Piacenza – Brescia), Cremona exit: take the ring road following the signs for Piacenza for about 10 km, then exit left into Via Trebbia. At the traffic lights turn into Contrada Santa Tecla; the campus is on the left.

From the A21, Castelvetro Piacentino exit: follow the signs for Cremona and cross the bridge over the Po. At the roundabout take the ring road towards Pavia and the first exit on the right (Via Trebbia). At the traffic lights turn into Contrada Santa Tecla; the campus is on the left.

Parking

  • Santa Tecla car park, Via Bissolati 93: about 80 spaces, 2 minutes on foot from the campus
  • Massarotti covered car park, Via Massarotti 19: about 200 spaces, 5 minutes on foot

The campus car park in Contrada Santa Tecla 7A is reserved for authorised guests.
